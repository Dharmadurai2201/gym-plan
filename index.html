Avoid these

  • 200 °C or higher: the turmeric and chilli burn black before the inside cooks
  • Too little yogurt: yogurt does the tenderising; don't drop below the amounts above
  • No oil in the marinade: the paste burns faster and doesn't cling
  • Pieces touching: they steam instead of crisping

Tips

  • Small 1-inch cubes: take 2 minutes off each side
  • Past 80 °C inside, breast dries out; a cheap kitchen thermometer pays for itself
  • If Thu and Fri pieces turn soft or chalky, leave the lemon out of the frozen bags and squeeze it on after cooking
  • Office reheat: 30–40 s in the microwave at most, or eat at room temperature
  • A perforated air-fryer parchment liner means almost no scrubbing

My day: gym, 2 hours of learning, 2–11 pm shift

Your sharpest hours are late morning, after training and breakfast, before work starts pulling at you. That's where the 2-hour learning block goes. Sleep is 12:30 to 8:30, the same on every day of the week, including weekends.

  1. 08:30
    Wake up
    Drink 500 ml water. Step outside for 5–10 minutes of daylight; it sets your body clock so 12:30 sleep comes easily. Mon, Wed, Fri: weigh in after the toilet, before coffee. Then black coffee, banana and honey.
  2. 09:00
    Gym, about 75 min
    Today's card from the Workouts tab. Creatine in your water bottle.
  3. 10:20
    Cook, shake, shower, breakfast at 11:00
    Chicken into the air fryer and eggs into the egg boiler first; shake and shower while they cook, then today's veg batch. At about 10:50, box everything and pack the snack box. Oats are already soaked.
  4. 11:20
    Learning block 1 · 50 min
    Hardest new material: a course module, a chapter, a tough concept. Phone in another room, notifications off.
  5. 12:10
    Break · 10 min
    Stand up, walk, refill water. No phone scrolling; it eats the break.
  6. 12:20
    Learning block 2 · 50 min
    Practice what you learned in block 1: build it, solve problems, do the lab. Doing beats rereading.
  7. 13:10
    Wrap-up · 10 min
    Write 3 lines: what you learned, what's unclear, what's next tomorrow.
  8. 13:20
    Grab your bag, leave at 1:30
    Slice cucumber and carrot into the box, take it from the fridge with the snack and a 1 L water bottle.
  9. 14:00
    Shift (Mon–Fri)
    Stand or walk 5 minutes every hour. Finish 2 bottles of water. 2:00 chicken box. 5:00 fruit snack. 8:00 office dinner, then a 10-minute walk. No coffee or tea after 6 pm.
  10. 23:30
    Home: wind down and pack
    Warm shower. Soak tomorrow's oats. Move tomorrow's chicken bag to the fridge. Pack the gym bag. Reread today's 3-line note (2 minutes, on paper).
  11. 00:00
    Screens off, lights dim
    Phone charging outside reach. Same time every night.
  12. 00:30
    Sleep · 8 hours
    One short night cut the muscle-building response by about 18% in a study. Protect this before anything else.

Rest days (Wed and Sun; Sun only on the 6-day plan)

  • Same 8:30 wake-up and daylight.
  • Learning moves earlier: 9:30–11:30, plus an optional third block 12:00–12:50.
  • A 30-minute walk replaces the gym.

Sunday reset

  • Morning: learning, 2–3 hours.
  • Evening, 20 min: check your weight average, look at the week's 3-line notes, and pick next week's learning goals.

Making 2 hours a day count

  • Test yourself, don't reread. Close the material and write what you remember, then check. This is the most reliable study method in the research.
  • Space it. Revisit each topic after 1 day, 3 days and 1 week. The Sunday review does this for you.
  • One topic a week. Switching subjects daily feels busy but builds little.
  • Track hours. Aim for 14 a week. A missed day is fine; two in a row means the plan needs changing.
